## About

Third-year Computer Science and Engineering student from Kerala, India, aspiring to build a career in software engineering. I learn by building real products: full-stack web platforms, privacy-first browser tools, developer utilities, mobile apps, and security tools.

I am the founder and lead developer of [evidebus.com](https://evidebus.com), a community-driven Kerala public transit platform with 168K+ users, and of [toolkitgen.com](https://toolkitgen.com), a free platform of 190+ browser-based utilities that run entirely on the user's device. I am also the MuLearn Cybersecurity Lead at my college, an official Google Student Ambassador, and a Google Developer Program member.

**Open to:** Software engineering internships, entry-level developer roles, cybersecurity collaborations, and open-source contributions.
